About

Where numbers
meet strategy.

Project Finance Fundraising & IPO Startup Incubation Sustainability

I work at the intersection of finance, fundraising, and compliance — helping businesses translate ideas and operations into numbers that hold up under scrutiny.

My work includes building financial models and DPRs that support funding decisions, preparing investor documentation, and advising on funding strategy and structuring for startups and growing businesses alike.

I've supported IPO preparation and compliance processes, and worked closely with senior officials at leading private and PSU banks to help move financing conversations forward. Beyond fundraising, I assess project feasibility and funding structures to help businesses make sound, well-informed decisions.

My foundation includes tax compliance, audit readiness, and internal controls — along with experience improving operational efficiency. Across all of this, my focus stays the same: helping businesses get their numbers right, their funding sorted, and their next steps clear.

I have been closely involved in the ideation and early-stage development of ventures in the finance and marketing space — contributing to their structure, strategy, and financial groundwork from the very first step.
